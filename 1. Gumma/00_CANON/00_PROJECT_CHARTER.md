# PROJECT GUMMA UNIVERSE — Project Charter
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Accepted Canon Protocol  
**Role Binding:** Lead Worldbuilding Engineer

---

## Purpose

Define the non-negotiable mission, scope, quality bar, and working method for Project Gumma Universe. This charter governs all subsequent documentation. It is not narrative prose; it is the constitution of the story bible.

---

## Assumptions

1. This repository is the sole official canon for Project Gumma Universe.
2. Documentation precedes storytelling. Chapters are produced only on explicit instruction.
3. The Gumma are a uniquely Indian secret organization — not ghosts, not supernatural beings, not Japanese ninjas.
4. Historical integration must remain plausible; invention may fill gaps but must never contradict accepted canon or casually rewrite settled real-world history without deliberate, flagged alternate-history rules.
5. Weak ideas will be challenged. Agreement without scrutiny is a failure mode.

---

## Known Facts (Locked by Charter)

### Identity Negatives (Hard Locks)
- The Gumma are **not** ghosts.
- The Gumma are **not** supernatural beings.
- The Gumma are **not** Japanese ninjas (nor a ninja analogue with Indian paint).

### Identity Positives (Directional Locks)
- The Gumma are an original Indian secret organization with distinct philosophy, history, methods, traditions, architecture, symbolism, and identity.
- End-state reader reaction target: *"The Gumma are unlike anything I've read before."*

### Production Scope (What We Build)
Universe Bible · Timeline · Historical Integration · Character Database · House Architecture · Underground Infrastructure · Organizational Hierarchy · Secret Traditions · Philosophy · Moral Code · Symbols · Maps · Family Trees · Political Influence · Languages · Training Systems · Weapons · Technologies · Hidden History · Long-term Story Seeds

### Production Scope (What We Do Not Build by Default)
- Novel chapters
- Screenplay pages
- Fan-service lore dumps without structural purpose

### Document Standard (Mandatory Sections)
Every canon document must include:
1. **Purpose**
2. **Assumptions**
3. **Known Facts**
4. **Unknown Questions**
5. **Possible Improvements**
6. **Connections to Existing Canon**
7. **Future Story Opportunities**

### Quality Gates
Every accepted entry must satisfy:
- Originality
- Believability
- Emotional Depth
- Historical Plausibility
- Internal Consistency
- Long-Term Franchise Potential

### Method Rules
- Never invent facts that contradict previously accepted canon.
- If an idea resembles another franchise, name the resemblance, explain the risk, and propose a more original alternative.
- When uncertain, ask questions instead of making assumptions.
- Challenge weak ideas respectfully.
- Detect inconsistencies as a primary duty.
- Expertise posture: historian, architect, anthropologist, linguist, military strategist, philosopher, psychologist, developmental editor.

### Inspiration Discipline (Not Imitation)
Inspired by the *discipline* of:
- Tolkien — depth, internal consistency, linguistic/cultural weight
- Doyle — mystery construction, clue architecture, controlled revelation
- Sanderson — long-term planning, systems that pay off
- Nolan — cinematic structure, temporal/causal elegance

These are method inspirations. Surface aesthetics, plots, orders, and symbols must remain original.

## Unknown Questions

1. Who holds final canon authority when creative disputes arise (single author, committee, Lead Worldbuilding Engineer veto)?
2. What is the primary delivery medium for the franchise (novels, film/TV, games, hybrid)?
3. What is the intended audience age band and tonal register (prestige thriller, epic historical, intimate family saga, etc.)?
4. How aggressively may documents invent until foundational questions in `01_FOUNDATIONS/01_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` are answered?
5. Will real historical figures appear as canon participants, background texture only, or never?

## Possible Improvements

1. Add a **Canon Status Taxonomy** (`Proposed` / `Provisional` / `Accepted` / `Locked` / `Deprecated`) to every document header.
2. Add a **Contradiction Log** workflow for tracking disputes and resolutions.
3. Add a **Sensitivity & Cultural Review** checklist for Indian regional, linguistic, caste, religious, and colonial-era material.
4. Define a **Naming Authority** process for etymology and multilingual terms before they proliferate.

## Future Story Opportunities

- Charter clarity enables multi-writer expansion without tonal collapse.
- Locked negatives (not ghosts / not supernatural / not ninjas) create a productive design pressure: the Gumma must earn mystique through culture, craft, secrecy, and consequence — not magic shortcuts.
- Method inspirations (Tolkien/Doyle/Sanderson/Nolan) can later map to specific bible modules (languages; clue ledgers; magic/tech systems analogue; nested timeline documents).
