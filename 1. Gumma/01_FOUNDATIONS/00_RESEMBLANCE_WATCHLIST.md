# Resemblance Watchlist
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Provisional  
**Last Reviewed:** 2026-08-02

---

## Purpose

Prevent the Gumma from collapsing into a familiar secret-society template wearing Indian costume. Inspiration is allowed; substitutable clones are not.

---

## Assumptions

1. Readers pattern-match aggressively; if the Gumma *play* like ninjas, calling them Indian will not save originality.
2. Naming a resemblance early is healthier than discovering it after fifty documents.
3. Differentiation must be structural (incentives, costs, culture, history), not cosmetic (different clothes/weapons).

---

## Known Facts

### High-Risk Comparables (Pre-Lore)

| Template | Why It Threatens Us | Structural Differentiation Demand |
|----------|---------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Japanese ninja / shinobi fiction | Charter explicitly forbids this lane | No clan-village stealth caste; no “shadow warrior” aesthetic as identity core |
| Hashashin / Assassin tropes | “Secret order that kills for ideology” is overfitted | If killing exists, it cannot be the brand; define rarer, costlier sanctions |
| Illuminati / global cabal | Omnipotent conspiracy flattens drama | Finite reach, local knowledge superiority, real logistical limits |
| Bene Gesserit | Breeding programs + whispered politics = instant Dune echo | Avoid prophetic genetics; prefer craft guilds, memory institutions, or legal shadow arbitration |
| SPECTRE / comic supervillain syndicates | Power fantasy without culture | Gumma must feel like a civilization fragment, not a villain boardroom |
| “Bogeyman is secretly real order” | Clever once; often twee | If folk *gumma* camouflage is used, it needs anthropological seriousness and social cost |
| Temple-guardian mystic warriors | Exoticism trap | Spiritual vocabulary ≠ supernatural powers; keep materialist craft rigor |

### Differentiation Pillars (To Be Filled After Open Questions)

1. **Purpose scarcity** — What only they do, that no state or gang does as well.
2. **Cost engine** — What membership destroys in the person (family, name, sleep, caste standing, memory, freedom).
3. **Geographic grain** — Specific Indian landscapes, languages, built forms — not generic “East.”
4. **Historical scar** — A founding wound that still shapes policy.
5. **Moral illegibility** — Readers should argue about whether the Gumma are justified.
