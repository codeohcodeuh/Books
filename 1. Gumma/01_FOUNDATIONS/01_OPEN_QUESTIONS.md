# Foundational Open Questions
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Provisional (blocking document)  
**Last Reviewed:** 2026-08-02

---

## Purpose

Identify load-bearing creative decisions that must be answered before deep lore is Accepted. Inventing a full legendarium atop unset foundations will produce elegant-looking inconsistency.

---

## Assumptions

1. Silence is not consent; unanswered questions remain Unknown.
2. Partial answers are welcome; they should be marked Provisional.
3. Some questions may remain permanently open as *reader mysteries* — but those must be conscious design choices, not author confusion.

---

## Known Facts

- Repository currently contains protocol documents only; no Gumma origin, hierarchy, or geography has been Accepted.
- Charter locks three negatives: not ghosts, not supernatural, not Japanese ninjas.
- Real-world linguistic note (external research cue, not yet canon): in parts of South India, *gumma* is a colloquial bogeyman figure used to frighten children. This may be a powerful cover-myth opportunity — or a cliché trap if handled as “the scary name is secretly an order.” **Not Accepted as canon.** Decision required.

## Unknown Questions

### A. Identity Core
1. **What are the Gumma, in one ruthless sentence?** (Function in the world: protectors, brokers, judges, archivists, saboteurs, something else?)
2. **What do they believe they are for?** (Self-mythology vs actual function may differ — do you want that gap?)
3. **Why “Gumma”?** In-universe etymology? Exonym? Cover name? Acronym-like compression? Deliberate folk-myth camouflage?
4. **What must never be true of them?** Beyond the three charter negatives — e.g., no immortality, no caste monopoly, no single-religion ownership, etc.

### B. Geography & Culture
5. **Where is the civilizational heartland?** Single region (e.g., Deccan, Bengal, Rajasthan, Kerala coast, Indo-Gangetic) or a polycentric network?
6. **Which languages shape their inner tongue and ritual speech?**
7. **How do they relate to India’s religious plurality?** Syncretic? Strictly secular craft guild? Multi-faith houses under one code?
8. **Urban vs rural power base?** Temples, ports, courts, bazaars, forests, modern metros — where does influence actually live?

### C. Time & History
9. **When do they begin?** Mythic antiquity claim vs historically plausible founding window?
10. **What is the “present day” of the primary franchise timeline?**
11. **How do they survive major ruptures?** Delhi Sultanate transitions, Mughal consolidation, European companies, 1857, Partition, Emergency, liberalization — which eras are load-bearing for their evolution?
12. **Alternate history policy:** soft integration (hidden influence inside real events) or hard divergence (public history changes)?

### D. Organization & Power
13. **Recruitment model:** bloodline houses, abducted orphans, voluntary induction, debt bondage, merit exams, mixed?
14. **Centralized command or federated houses?**
15. **What do they control that makes them matter?** Information, logistics, assassination, arbitration, money, memory, infrastructure?
16. **Who are their natural antagonists?** State intelligence, rival orders, criminal syndicates, colonial offices, ideological movements, internal schisms?

### E. Tone, Medium, Audience
17. **Primary medium?** Novel cycle / film-TV / games / transmedia?
18. **Tonal north star?** Cold procedural? Family tragedy? Geopolitical thriller? Quiet anthropological epic?
19. **Violence ethic on-page/on-screen?** Precise and rare, or systemic and costly?
20. **How much should readers admire them?** Heroes, anti-heroes, necessary monsters, or contested inheritance?

### F. Systems (Sanderson Discipline Without Magic)
21. **If they have “signature capabilities,” what are the non-magical sources?** Training, architecture, cryptography, pharmacology, social engineering, cartography, acoustics, finance?
22. **What is the cost of those capabilities?** (Every advantage needs a price, or they become ninjas-with-henna.)
23. **What mystery is the franchise’s long spine?** One central withheld truth, or a nested ledger of reveals?

### G. Canon Process
24. **Who has final say on Accepted canon?**
25. **May I propose Provisional answers for you to accept/reject in batches, or do you want question-by-question control?**
