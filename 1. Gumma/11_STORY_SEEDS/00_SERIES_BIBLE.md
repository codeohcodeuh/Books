## Purpose

Track what the philosophical canon cannot: recurring characters, ages, locations, timelines, unresolved promises, revealed vs unrevealed institutional knowledge, and continuity across novels.

- **Canon** = constitution (what is true).  
- **Series Bible** = operations (what has been *shown*, to whom, and what must stay consistent in fiction).

If fiction conflicts with Accepted/Locked canon, fiction yields or the contradiction is logged in `00_CANON/02_CONTRADICTION_LOG.md`.

---

## Assumptions

1. Readers fall in love with ordinary people first; the institution is noticed late.
2. Each novel may reveal only a controlled slice of institutional truth.
3. Characters age in real time across books unless a book is explicitly historical.
4. Unresolved promises are assets — do not tidy them for comfort.
5. The word “Gumma” is a nuclear reveal; treat it as series-level ammunition, not Book 1 vocabulary.

# 1. FRANCHISE STORYTELLING NORTH STAR

> Readers should not fall in love with the Gumma first.  
> They should fall in love with ordinary people living ordinary lives — and only later realize the invisible architecture was present all along.

**Emotional franchise thesis:**  
Civilization survives because someone remembers.

**Genre:** Literary civilizational fiction (not fantasy; not thriller-first; mystery as texture).

**Hard locks (storytelling):**
- No supernatural powers
- No prophecy / chosen one
- No secret gadgets / magical archives
- No on-page institutional lectures
- Chapters begin with human problems

# 8. CONTINUITY RULES FOR DRAFTING

1. **Age math:** Update registry when a book’s present year locks.
2. **Travel time:** Bengaluru ↔ Maddur belt must remain realistic (bus/train/car hours).
3. **Language:** Kannada-first social world; English in office; do not exoticize dialogue with fake “broken English.”
4. **Money:** No mysterious wealth around G characters; ordinary clothes, ordinary transport.
5. **Phones exist.** Gumma characters use them banally; substantive holdings still face-to-face (canon). Don’t show them WhatsApping agreement terms.
6. **Police/courts:** Available, imperfect, not cartoonishly corrupt every scene.
7. **Caste/community:** Specific, non-lecturing, non-essentialist; avoid treating communities as monoliths.
8. **Recurring props:** The path; Lakshmi’s stopped sentence; Farooq’s shop bench — treat as series furniture.
9. **No cool fight scenes.** De-escalation is the spectacular thing.
10. After each drafted chapter: log new promises/reveals in this bible before drafting the next.

---

# 9. TONE & PROSE CONTRACT

- Close third; patient; observant; human.
- Humor allowed (wedding logistics, apartment WhatsApp) without sitcom.
- Grief without orchestration.
- Sex/romance: adult if present; never as initiation metaphor.
- Violence: rare, consequential, unglamorous.
- Metaphors: prefer agricultural, municipal, domestic — not occult.

**Ban list for prose:** chosen, destiny, ancient order (as narration), “for centuries we have…”, glowing archives, hooded figures, sudden martial competence.
