## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Introduce the state apparatus — and its insufficiency. Inspector Priya takes statements from both sides; they contradict; she can smell something older beneath the shove but her tools (FIR, statements, survey marks) cannot reach it. Establish the law as documentation-after-fact, not prevention. Show that the state's resolution mechanisms don't reach the social roots.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
Police thread has no origin scene; Ch.27 file-closure has no setup; Priya has no introduction; the novel's argument about state-vs-social-order never concretizes; reader doesn't see law's frustrating mismatch with reality.

**Promise job:**  
P-POLICE advances (complaint filed; investigation active). The legal clock starts.

**Emotional job:**  
Unease. The procedural variety — filling forms that cannot capture what actually happened; feeling a social conflict through the wrong instrument.

**Institutional / RP job:**  
0 RP. Priya is state, not Gumma. Her presence highlights what formal law CAN do (document, threaten, summon) and what it CANNOT (resolve shared-use history; repair trust; prevent next incident). The gap between state and social order is the space the invisible institution occupies — but the novel doesn't say this.

**Prose must avoid:**  
Police-procedural genre conventions; making Priya a detective; explaining Indian legal system; making this a courtroom setup; editorializing about state failure.

**Forbidden exposition:**  
IPC sections explained; how FIRs work; police hierarchy; path legal history; survey/revenue system.

**Forbidden lore:**  
All institutional content. "If only someone remembered" never appears.

**Forbidden vocabulary (Manual Appendix B + chapter-specific):**

**Invisible layer limits:**  
Zero stated. The absence is only visible in Priya's frustrated arrows — her tools can't reach what she senses.

**POV restrictions:**  
Close-third Priya. Ramesh and Prasad perceived externally during statements (she reads them professionally — edits, tells, anxiety). No access to their interiors from her chapter.

**Dialogue restrictions:**  
Statement-giving register (careful, edited, formal). Priya asks procedural questions. Constable offers one practical observation. No one philosophizes. No one explains history beyond immediate claims.

**Allowed ambiguity:**  
Whether Priya's "something underneath" intuition will lead anywhere; whether police presence will deter or escalate; whose statement is closer to truth.
