## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Emotional close. Meera leaves the village. This time, departure costs her — because she BELONGS now in a way she didn't at Ch6 arrival. The chapter is about the grief of leaving something you've earned through pain, presence, and partial understanding. She doesn't have answers. She has: one frame (Lakshmi's sentence), overlapping footprints, the smell of stored blankets, and a phone that will ping soon with a path photo from Ananya.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
Novel has no emotional close; Meera's arc (exile → belonging → departure-with-grief) never completes; P-LEAVE never closes; the reader doesn't feel the cost of belonging; the phone-glow image (final novel image before Coda) is lost.

**Promise job:**  
P-LEAVE closes. The departure IS the payoff of belonging — it only hurts because she earned it.

**Emotional job:**  
Grief — the clean kind. Not melodrama. The specific grief of leaving a place that now holds you, knowing you'll return but not knowing when, carrying more than you came with.

## 3–4. Scenes

**S01 (6:00–8:00 a.m., Hegde house):** Departure morning. Bag packed. Goodbyes. Lakshmi: brief, warm, hand on face (same gesture as arrival Ch6 — bookend). Ramesh: awkward but warmer than Ch7 — he nods at her differently now. "Come sooner next time." (From the man who barely saw her before.) Saroja: logistics-as-love (bus timing, water bottle, phone charged?). The guest room: blanket folded (callback Ch6). Shankarappa's room: empty again — he left two days ago, quietly, no goodbye. His absence this time feels different: not abandonment but: his work here is done, for now. Meera notices: his sandals spot by the door is bare.

**S02 (9:00–11:00 a.m., bus stand + bus):** Bus stand. Farooq waves from store ("Come back, city girl!"). Bus boarding. Rain starting properly — monsoon's first real commitment. As bus pulls away, phone pings: Ananya sent a photo. The path. After rain. Mud. Green. Footprints already new (today's — farmers walking normally). The image: the path looking like it always was — shared, ordinary, unremarkable. Which means: it worked. The dispute is absorbed into normalcy. No monument. No plaque. Just: a path that both families use. The phone glow of this image in the moving bus = novel's final image (before Coda shifts to city).
