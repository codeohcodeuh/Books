## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Prove by negative space that Shankarappa's presence was functional. During his three-day absence, rumor escalates, the path dispute heats up again (new WhatsApp forwards claiming Hegde-side is "planning retaliation"), community temperature rises. No one connects this to the quiet man's departure — but the reader should feel: when he was here, this didn't happen. His absence is the evidence of his function.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
The absence has no consequence; Ch25 return has no relief-contrast; the reader never feels what the institution prevents; the "proof by absence" argument (core to the novel's structure) is lost.

**Promise job:**  
P-ABSENCE advances — the absence teaches.

**Emotional job:**  
Dread. The slow kind: watching a situation deteriorate when the invisible brake has been removed.

**Institutional / RP job:**  
0 RP. Shankarappa is absent. The institutional layer is proved by its absence — not by any present action. This is structural argument, not textual reveal.
