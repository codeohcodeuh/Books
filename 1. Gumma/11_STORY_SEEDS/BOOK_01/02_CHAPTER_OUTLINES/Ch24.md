## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
A traveler passes through Farooq's store and delivers one sentence to Farooq in Meera's presence. The sentence is opaque (Meera hears it but can't parse its meaning: it sounds like an oblique message, not a conversation). The traveler leaves. Farooq pauses — something crossed his face — then resumes normality. Meera witnesses the ecology of non-digital communication. P-MSG opens as a series-level mystery. **Human-first:** errands and rice-bags before any network texture. **Agency:** Meera chooses not to ask Farooq.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
The Walker function never hints; the message ecology never appears; P-MSG never opens; the novel loses its single evidence that a NETWORK exists (not just individuals); Farooq's node-function stays invisible.

**Promise job:**  
P-MSG opens — the reader glimpses a communication system. Not explained. Not resolved. Series-level.

**Emotional job:**  
Unease — the cold kind. Seeing something move through a system you can't map.

**Institutional / RP job:**  
**1 RP (R5).** On-page: traveler delivers one sentence to Farooq; Farooq pauses; traveler leaves. Reader interprets: "Gossip network" or "conspiracy courier." Incorrect: thriller-courier; confirmed spy. Correct (writer only): Walker function — message ecology. One node to another. Content unclear by design.
