## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Introduce Ramesh and the path dispute through physical discovery — wire moved, boundary encroached. Establish the novel's central concrete conflict: who owns the cart-path between Hegde and Gowda fields? Anchor reader in village geography, seasonal rhythm, and the pride that makes men interpret fencing as insult.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
Path dispute has no genesis scene; Ramesh has no introduction; the novel's central human conflict arrives unnamed; reader has no sense of village dawn, fieldwork, or land-body relationship.

**Promise job:**  
P-PATH opens as crisis. The reader now knows: there is a disputed path; wire has moved; this matters to Ramesh more than reason can explain.

**Emotional job:**  
Pride. The specific variety that turns a boundary dispute into an identity question — "this is about my father's walk, not about meters."

**Institutional / RP job:**  
0 RP. Purely civilian. No invisible layer. Ramesh's dispute is his own — no rememberer present, no memory invoked. The absence of institutional memory is the condition that allows this to escalate. Writer knows this; text does not.

## 10. Drafting Constraints

**Prose must avoid:**  
Romanticizing rural life; making Ramesh noble-farmer archetype; narrator taking sides on the boundary; explaining land law; agricultural lecture.

**Forbidden exposition:**  
Karnataka land-ownership systems; history of the path; 1962 understanding; Venki's perspective on the wire; wedding alliance details beyond what Ramesh feels.

**Forbidden lore:**  
All institutional content. No rememberers. No past mediators. No historical references beyond Ramesh's vague sense of "always."

**Forbidden vocabulary (Manual Appendix B + chapter-specific):**  
ancient order · chosen · destiny · for centuries · secret society · Holder · Verifier · Connector · Walker · Transmitter · Covenant · Article · Gumma · civilizational memory · mystical · supernatural · steward · custodian · rememberer · invisible · 1962 · formal agreement · terms (in treaty sense)

**Invisible layer limits:**  
Zero. Entirely absent. Not even a void-hint.

**POV restrictions:**  
Close-third Ramesh only. Gowda side completely opaque — Ramesh cannot know who ordered the wire move. His interpretation is his, possibly wrong.

**Dialogue restrictions:**  
Almost zero dialogue. Worker greeting exchange only (physical nod + one word). Ramesh's chapter is internal and physical. No one he talks to. This is a solitary discovery chapter.

**Allowed ambiguity:**  
Whether Venki ordered the shift or farmhand overstepped; whether Ramesh's pride is justified or territorial; whether silence here is wisdom or the seed of escalation.
