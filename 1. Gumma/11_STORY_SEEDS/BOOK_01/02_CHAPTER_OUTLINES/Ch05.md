## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Introduce Ananya — the bride — and ground wedding-as-emotional-reality rather than plot-device. Establish her cheerfulness, her competence, and the crack beneath: she overhears the men's path-talk and realizes her wedding is being used as leverage in a fight she didn't ask to inherit. Plant the anger she will swallow until Ch.13.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
Ananya is a paper figure; the wedding is an abstraction; Ch.13's ceremony-choosing loses its setup; the bride's private fury has no origin scene; reader never sees the wedding as a woman's experience.

**Promise job:**  
P-WED advances — wedding becomes physically real (henna, fabrics, lists). No new promise opened; existing clock advances with texture.

**Emotional job:**  
Anticipation — the specific bride's version: excitement, fear, logistics overwhelm, and the private anger of discovering your ceremony has become someone else's chess move.

**Institutional / RP job:**  
0 RP. Purely civilian. Family dynamics; wedding logistics; overheard argument.

## 3. Opening State

**POV entering emotion:**  
Genuine excitement. Ananya has been planning her mehndi trial with friends. She's happy. The logistics are hers to manage; she's good at this.

**POV entering knowledge:**  
Ananya knows: wedding in 15 days; families are aligned; some tension between her father-figure Ramesh and Venki-side exists (background noise — every wedding has this). She does NOT know about the wire shift specifically.

**Other characters — entering emotions:**
- Revathi (aunt): full-steam logistics; joyful-bossy
- Malathi (teacher colleague; friend): supportive; helping with henna trial
- Ramesh (offstage → briefly overheard): tight-voiced; meeting someone in back room about "the path matter"
- Saroja (offstage call): confirming Meera arrival

**Unresolved pressure entering:**  
Ch4's wire discovery (Ramesh holding it; Ananya doesn't know). Wedding logistics (normal pressure). Family image maintenance.

**Reader assumptions currently true:**  
Path dispute is simmering beneath wedding; Meera is booked to arrive tomorrow; Ananya is the bride.

**Reader assumptions we will not correct this chapter:**  
That path-talk is manageable background and won't poison the wedding.

## 10. Drafting Constraints

**Prose must avoid:**  
Making Ananya only-angry (she must be joyful first, genuinely); reducing wedding prep to tedious list; making Ramesh's words too clear (must be fragmentary).

**Forbidden exposition:**  
Path dispute details; 1962 terms; village land history; how weddings work in Karnataka; henna-as-cultural-explainer.

**Forbidden lore:**  
All institutional content.

**Forbidden vocabulary (Manual Appendix B + chapter-specific):**  
ancient order · chosen · destiny · for centuries · secret society · Holder · Verifier · Connector · Walker · Transmitter · Covenant · Article · Gumma · civilizational memory · mystical · supernatural · steward · custodian · rememberer · invisible · mediator (institutional sense) · 1962

**Invisible layer limits:**  
Zero. Not even structurally hinted.

**POV restrictions:**  
Close-third Ananya only. Ramesh perceived as overheard voice (no access to his reasoning). Other women perceived externally but warmly.

**Dialogue restrictions:**  
Women's dialogue: logistics, patterns, family jokes, phone interruptions. Ramesh's words: fragmentary, muffled — NOT full sentences reader can parse into plot. Ananya has NO confrontation dialogue.

**Allowed ambiguity:**  
Whether Ramesh is being unreasonable or protecting legitimate interests — reader doesn't have enough to judge yet (and won't until Ch.16). Whether Ananya will confront or keep swallowing.
