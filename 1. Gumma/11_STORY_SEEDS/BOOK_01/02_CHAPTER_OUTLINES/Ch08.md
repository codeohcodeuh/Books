## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Introduce Suresh and the clinic-as-confessional. Show how a local doctor becomes unwilling mediator for community stress. Plant P-DOC — a deliberate misdirection where Suresh pauses, sends a text to someone offstage, and the reader is invited to suspect he's "one of them." He isn't. He's just a local doctor texting his wife about being late.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
Suresh has no introduction; the misdirection pattern (reader suspects wrong person) never plants; the taluk-town geography is unestablished; the novel loses its "wrong texture" civilian red herring.

**Promise job:**  
P-DOC opens — reader sees a pause and a text. This misdirection runs through the novel (reader suspects Suresh is institutional; he isn't — wrong texture, confirmed by his messiness/boundaries-failing). Will retire gracefully by end of novel.

**Emotional job:**  
Unease. The specific exhaustion of being told things you can't use, fix, or unsee — the confessional without absolution.

**Institutional / RP job:**  
**0 RP by design.** This is misdirection. The pause + text is NOT institutional communication — it's a tired man texting his wife. But it's staged to look like something more. Blueprint note: "Do not score doctor's text as network."

## 10. Drafting Constraints

**Prose must avoid:**  
Making Suresh wise/profound; medical jargon dump; narrator explaining that the text is or isn't institutional; resolving P-DOC in either direction this chapter.

**Forbidden exposition:**  
Medical systems in India; how clinics work; Suresh's training history; Gowda family tree; path dispute details via the nephew (keep it feeling-level, not factual).

**Forbidden lore:**  
All institutional content.

**Forbidden vocabulary (Manual Appendix B + chapter-specific):**  
ancient order · chosen · destiny · for centuries · secret society · Holder · Verifier · Connector · Walker · Transmitter · Covenant · Article · Gumma · civilizational memory · mystical · supernatural · steward · custodian · rememberer · invisible · network · contact · handler · report · duty (institutional sense) · inform

**Invisible layer limits:**  
Zero real. The misdirection looks like one but isn't. Narrator never confirms or denies. The text's content stays hidden. The staging is ambiguous by design.

**POV restrictions:**  
Close-third Suresh. His interior: we know what the text says (to wife, about lunch). But the prose will NOT state "he texted his wife" — it will state "he texted" and "put the phone face-down." The reader's uncertainty is structural.

**IMPORTANT CRAFT NOTE:** The withholding is done via selectivity, not lying. Suresh's POV knows who he's texting. The prose simply doesn't specify. This is narrator selectivity, not unreliable narration.

**Dialogue restrictions:**  
Nephew speaks in anxious ramble (specific enough to feel real, vague enough not to be exposition). Suresh responds in doctor-calm platitudes. No one mentions the path directly — nephew says "tension between the families" / "what if it all goes wrong." Generic enough to be medical-adjacent.

**Allowed ambiguity:**  
Who the text is to; whether Suresh has a role beyond medicine; whether his platitude was generic or knowing.
