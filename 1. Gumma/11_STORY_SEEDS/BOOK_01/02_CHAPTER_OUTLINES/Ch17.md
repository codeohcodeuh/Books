## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Show shame from the inside. Ramesh processes the 1962 revelation: his "our path" narrative was wrong. His father's word — which he'd built identity around — was "let the path be wider than our pride." He has been the pride. He IS what his father warned against. This chapter is Ramesh's crack. The field-walk is his processing space. Arjun calls and says "just put it in writing" — the modern son's instinct — and Ramesh is caught between old shame and new temptation.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
Ramesh's internal response to Ch16 is absent; P-PAPER never opens; Arjun's ideology has no scene; the pride → humiliation arc doesn't get its private moment; Ch.25 (return) has no contrition foundation.

**Promise job:**  
P-PAPER opens — the temptation to convert oral memory into legal document (which would solve nothing, as the novel will show). P-PATH continues in new phase (post-truth).

**Emotional job:**  
Shame. The deep kind that comes from discovering you were the problem you blamed on others.

**Institutional / RP job:**  
0 RP. Shankarappa doesn't appear. The residue of his words lives in Ramesh's body.
