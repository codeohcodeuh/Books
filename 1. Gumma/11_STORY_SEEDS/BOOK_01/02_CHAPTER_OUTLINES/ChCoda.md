## 10. Drafting Constraints

**Prose must avoid:**  
Confirmation in ANY direction; memorable stranger; Meera following; narrator closure; theatrical ending; "and she knew" / "and she realized" — she **suspects**, **questions**, **holds**.

**Forbidden exposition:**  
Institutional explanation; village recap essay; Lakshmi sentence spoken aloud to stranger.

**Forbidden lore:**  
Appendix B absolutely zero. Reads as: woman on commute who notices something that might be nothing.

**Forbidden vocabulary:**  
Full nuclear set; "network"; "rememberer"; "Holder"; etc.

**Invisible layer limits:**  
**1 RP.** Ambiguity IS limit. Lean toward confirmation = fail. Lean toward denial = fail. Balance: "I think I just saw—" then nothing. Stranger already walking on.

**POV restrictions:**  
Close-third Meera. Stranger: silhouette, umbrella, direction, speed only. Arguing men: external.

**Dialogue restrictions:**  
**ZERO from Meera.** Stranger's word: **INAUDIBLE** (rain). Arguing men: brief voices before → confused silence after. Almost entirely interior + sensory.

**Allowed ambiguity:**  
EVERYTHING about incident. Meera changed by question itself — yes; that IS ending.

**ISS-17 lock — PUBLIC/AMBIENT WITHHOLD:**  
Distinct from Ch29 intimate relational stop. Here: crowd, rain, anonymous silhouette, city noise, no hand on head, no Ajji, no unfinished sentence spoken **to** Meera. Ambiguity is **environmental** — passerby, commute, might-be-nothing. Fail if prose feels like bedroom intimacy.
