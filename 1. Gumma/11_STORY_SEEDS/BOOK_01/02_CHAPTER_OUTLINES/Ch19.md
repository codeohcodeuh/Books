## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Comic relief with thematic function. The apartment AGM (parking, CCTV, maintenance fund) is the urban micro-version of the village path dispute — same human pattern (contested shared space; whose rules?) in a completely different costume. For the first time, Meera sees the rhyme herself: "This is the same fight. Smaller scale. Same structure." P-APT closes as truce (not victory, not friendship — pragmatic coexistence with Asha).

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
P-APT never closes; the urban-rural thematic rhyme never becomes conscious for Meera; the novel loses its comic-air beat at this position; reader has no breathing room between Ch18 loneliness and Ch20 new mystery.

**Promise job:**  
P-APT closes as truce. Not resolution; coexistence. Asha and Meera will never be friends. But the parking fight is over because it's absurd — and Meera now SEES it's absurd because she's lived the village version.

**Emotional job:**  
Amusement — the healing kind. The click of recognizing a pattern across scales. Brief, warm, slightly melancholic (because seeing the pattern means she's changed).

**Institutional / RP job:**  
0 RP. Purely civilian thematic work.
