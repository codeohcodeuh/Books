## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Wedding-eve gathering introduces Shankarappa into the novel through Meera's observing eyes. He is NOT named as anything special. He is a "dull guest" — quiet, older, unremarkable. But elders defer to him in micro-ways Meera notices. The chapter plants P-GUEST: who is this man, and why do people respect him without any visible reason? Simultaneously: wedding-eve emotions, both families under one roof with unresolved path-violence beneath.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
Shankarappa has no introduction; P-GUEST never plants; the wedding-eve emotional cocktail (forced joy + buried anger + alliance under pressure) never shows; Ch.16 arrives without reader knowing this quiet man exists.

**Promise job:**  
P-GUEST opens — a puzzle of respect without visible cause. P-WED advances to eve.

**Emotional job:**  
Unease beneath festivity. The specific feeling of a family gathering where everyone is performing happiness while bruised underneath.

**Institutional / RP job:**  
**0 RP** by design. Shankarappa is present but does NOT demonstrate function. Elders' deference is the only signal — and it could mean anything (old friend? distant relative? money?). No institutional information transmitted. This is planting, not revealing.

## 10. Drafting Constraints

**Prose must avoid:**  
Making Shankarappa mysterious/atmospheric in narrator voice (he's perceived as dull — Meera's word); creating gothic corridor scenes; making the sandal-observation feel like a "clue"; signaling to reader "this is important."

**Forbidden exposition:**  
Who Shankarappa is/was; institutional function; why elders defer; Lakshmi's relationship to the institution; path-dispute connection to his presence.

**Forbidden lore:**  
All institutional content.

**Forbidden vocabulary (Manual Appendix B + chapter-specific):**  
ancient order · chosen · destiny · for centuries · secret society · Holder · Verifier · Connector · Walker · Transmitter · Covenant · Article · Gumma · civilizational memory · mystical · supernatural · steward · custodian · rememberer · invisible · keeper · watcher · authority · elder (in titled sense) · teacher (as role) · someone who knows

**Invisible layer limits:**  
Zero explicit. The deference is shown but unexplained. It must be possible to read it as normal elder-respect. The sandals are neat — they could be a personality trait. Lakshmi's door-closing could be ordinary bedtime. Every signal must have a plausible civilian reading.

**POV restrictions:**  
Close-third Meera. Shankarappa perceived entirely externally (body language, clothes, sandals, silence, others' reactions to him). NO access to his interior. Lakshmi perceived briefly in corridor — voice + door.

**Dialogue restrictions:**  
Minimal from Shankarappa (perhaps one greeting or acknowledgment to an elder — nothing that reveals). Lakshmi: two sentences max. Wedding-eve crowd: background murmur + specific snatches. No one discusses the path in this festive space (performative peace).

**Allowed ambiguity:**  
Whether Shankarappa is important or merely old; whether Lakshmi is protecting or dismissing; whether Meera will pursue her curiosity.
