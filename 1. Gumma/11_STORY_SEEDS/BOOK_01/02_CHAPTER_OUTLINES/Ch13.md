## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
The wedding happens. Not despite the path dispute — WITH it unresolved underneath. Ananya chooses the ceremony knowing the fathers haven't settled. This is her act of agency: choosing a marriage while aware that it begins on cracked ground. The chapter is the emotional peak of the first half — love existing in imperfect conditions. It completes P-WED as kept promise.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
The novel's central event is absent; Ananya's agency disappears; P-WED never closes; the emotional peak has no scene; the wedding exists only as reported not experienced.

**Promise job:**  
P-WED closes as KEPT. The wedding happens. Emotionally complete. The path dispute doesn't destroy it — but it doesn't vanish either. Marriage under strain, not marriage in ruins.

**Emotional job:**  
Belonging. The bravest kind: choosing to enter a system (marriage, family, alliance) knowing it's flawed, because the love is real even if the ground isn't clean.

**Institutional / RP job:**  
0 RP. Purely civilian. Shankarappa may be present (background, in the crowd) but does nothing visible. This is entirely Ananya's human story.

## 3. Opening State

**POV entering emotion:**  
Terrified joy. The morning is happening. This is real. She's getting married today.

**POV entering knowledge:**  
Ananya knows: fathers haven't resolved the path (she overheard Ch5; she's picked up more since); she knows Karthik loves her; she knows both families are performing; she's choosing to do this anyway. Her false belief ("clean rooms are found, not built") is being challenged by her own action — she's building this room while messy.

**Other characters — entering emotions:**

- Karthik (offstage until ceremony): nervous; genuine

**Unresolved pressure entering:**  
Path dispute (background hum); police file (open); fathers cold to each other; Shankarappa present as quiet observer (not visible to Ananya).

**Reader assumptions currently true:**  
Wedding will happen; dispute doesn't destroy everything; but strain persists.

**Reader assumptions we will not correct this chapter:**  
None. This chapter delivers what was promised — the wedding.

## 10. Drafting Constraints

**Prose must avoid:**  
Wedding as ethnography; explaining rituals for non-Indian reader; making ceremony generic (it must be specific — Kannada Brahmin or local tradition details from research); fathers stealing focus; the wedding as obstacle to plot rather than emotional peak; any series-level foreshadowing (DEC-J).

**Forbidden exposition:**  
Ritual explanations; caste context; marriage-law; family tree recap; path dispute details during ceremony.

**Forbidden lore:**  
All institutional content.

**Forbidden vocabulary (Manual Appendix B + chapter-specific):**  
ancient order · chosen (except: "she chose this") · destiny · for centuries · secret society · Holder · Verifier · Connector · Walker · Transmitter · Covenant · Article · Gumma · civilizational memory · mystical · supernatural · steward · custodian · rememberer · invisible

**Invisible layer limits:**  
Zero functional. Shankarappa present in crowd but NOT in Ananya's attention or prose except possibly as "guests seated" group. Writer knows he's there; text doesn't distinguish him.

**POV restrictions:**  
Close-third Ananya. She sees details selectively: jasmine smell, Karthik's hand, fathers' body language, Lakshmi's closed eyes, Meera in crowd. She does NOT catalog all guests. She does NOT analyze the political situation.

**Dialogue restrictions:**  
Almost zero. Priest chants (background sound). Revathi: one logistics shout in S01. Ramesh: overheard "no scenes today." Karthik and Ananya: no dialogue (they communicate through eyes and hands). Wedding is experienced, not discussed.

**DEC-J lock:** Do not tip Meera's series role, Shankarappa's survival/death, or mandatory Coda sequel payoff. Peak chapter stays human and closed on its own terms.

**Allowed ambiguity:**  
Whether the fathers' cold politeness will survive post-wedding; whether this marriage's foundation (cracked ground) will hold.
