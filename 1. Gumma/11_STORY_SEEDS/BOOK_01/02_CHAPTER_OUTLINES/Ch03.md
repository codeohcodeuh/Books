## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Activate the wedding clock that pulls Meera toward the village. Introduce Saroja's voice and the mother-daughter dynamic (loving friction, logistics-as-control). Establish that Meera's return is not voluntary curiosity but familial gravity — and that "uncle being difficult" is the first signal of the path dispute entering her awareness, however faintly.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
Meera arrives at the village without reader knowing why it's reluctant; Saroja exists without introduction; wedding has no clock; the "uncle difficult" thread never plants; emotional distinction between guest and willing participant never establishes.

**Promise job:**  
P-WED clock opens. Reader knows a wedding is coming; Meera must be there; family pressure is real but not cruel.

**Emotional job:**  
Anticipation — the specific dread-mixed-with-obligation of booking non-refundable travel to a family event you don't want to attend but cannot refuse.

**Institutional / RP job:**  
0 RP. Zero institutional presence. Pure family dynamics.

## 10. Drafting Constraints

**Prose must avoid:**  
Explaining mother-daughter dynamics as anthropological observation; making Saroja shrill; over-describing the booking UI; reflecting philosophically on family obligation.

**Forbidden exposition:**  
Path dispute details; village geography; Ramesh's actual situation; why Meera's father isn't coming (just "won't come" — background fact, not explained here).

**Forbidden lore:**  
All institutional content.

**Forbidden vocabulary (Manual Appendix B + chapter-specific):**  
ancient order · chosen · destiny · for centuries · secret society · Holder · Verifier · Connector · Walker · Transmitter · Covenant · Article · Gumma · civilizational memory · mystical · supernatural · initiation · steward · custodian · rememberer · invisible (institutional sense)

**Invisible layer limits:**  
Zero. Absent entirely.

**POV restrictions:**  
Close-third Meera only. Saroja perceived through voice (phone); no access to Saroja's interior.

**Dialogue restrictions:**  
Saroja speaks in logistics-as-love (dates, names, expectations). Meera speaks in minimal responses, occasional dry deflection. No one philosophizes about duty, tradition, or belonging. "Uncle difficult" is dropped casually by Saroja — one phrase, not repeated.

**Allowed ambiguity:**  
Whether Meera's reluctance is laziness, genuine alienation, or protective distance — reader reads their own experience in.
