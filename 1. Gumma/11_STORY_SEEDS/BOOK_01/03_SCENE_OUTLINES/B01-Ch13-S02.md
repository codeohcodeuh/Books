**Working title:** Laugh-Cry  
**Status:** READY FOR PROSE

---

## 1. Scene Objective
Deliver the wedding ceremony as emotional peak of the first half. Ananya and Karthik marry while the cracked ground persists underneath. The laugh-cry at the final circle closes P-WED as KEPT. Love exists in imperfect conditions.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Endurance → vulnerability → earned joy → laugh-cry (simultaneous; the sound that contains both).

## 3. Scene Conflict
The ceremony is beautiful and real — AND the fathers are meters apart refusing to look at each other, the police file is open, the path is unresolved. Ananya holds both truths through the ceremony. The heat, the length, the smoke, the weight of context — all endured because the love (Karthik's hand, warm, shaking) is also real.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
The ceremony's length and physical weight (heat, smoke, time). The visible coldness between fathers. The crowd performing normality. The knowledge that this marriage begins on a fault line. Karthik's vulnerability (shaking hand) — which makes her MORE determined, not less.
