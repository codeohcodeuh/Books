**Working title:** BP Is Fine  
**Status:** READY FOR PROSE  
**Version:** 1.0  

---

## 1. Scene Objective
Show Suresh's professional boundary dissolving as a Gowda nephew uses a BP check as pretext for anxiety about the family escalation. Establish Suresh as the town's accidental confessional — overwhelmed, kind, unable to fix what he hears.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Professional control → absorbed → exhausted → boundary-recovering.

## 3. Scene Conflict
Suresh wants to maintain 10-minute appointments and efficient clinic flow; the nephew's genuine distress extends the consultation to 25 minutes; Suresh cannot fix interpersonal politics with a stethoscope but cannot refuse to listen.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
The nephew is tearful and genuine; turning him away feels cruel; the medical cover (BP) is a fiction both know; the queue outside grows; Suresh's response must be kind without being wisdom.

- Suresh: doctor-calm. Minimal. "How long have you been feeling this way?" / "These things have happened before. They usually settle after events pass." — platitude as kindness, not wisdom.

- Allowed: "tension between the families" / "what if it all goes wrong" — generic, emotional.
