## 1. Scene Objective
Establish Ananya as joyful, competent, genuinely excited about her wedding — then crack that joy with a single overheard sound: Ramesh's raised voice about "the path." Her henna cone freezes mid-air.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Joyful busyness → alert freeze.

## 3. Scene Conflict
Ananya wants to enjoy wedding prep on her own terms; Revathi keeps overriding pattern choices; phone interruptions from Gowda-side MIL; then the deeper threat — Ramesh's voice from the back room, introducing a conflict she didn't ask to carry.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
Surface: Revathi's pattern changes; MIL questions. Real: Ramesh's muffled voice using her wedding as timeline. She cannot investigate from the hall without "making a scene."

## IV. Scene Purpose

**Why this scene exists:** Make Ananya real — joyful, competent, warm — before cracking the joy. Establish wedding prep as physical/sensory. Land the freeze that drives her to S02 investigation.

**Removal test:** Ananya's joy is never established (making later anger seem default); wedding prep stays abstract; the overhearing has no contrast against genuine happiness.

**Promise work:** P-WED advances through tangible prep (henna, fabrics, lists).

**Relationship work:** Ananya–Revathi 4 (warm comic); Ananya–Malathi established.

**Emotional work:** Joy → freeze.

**Institutional / RP work:** 0.
