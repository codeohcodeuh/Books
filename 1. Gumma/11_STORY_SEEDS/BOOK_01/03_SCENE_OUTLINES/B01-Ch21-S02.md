## 1. Scene Objective
Meera alone at the jatre's edge, processing why she closed the door. She can't fully explain her protective instinct. The loudspeaker-distortion moment provides a half-insight: "Some things only work when they're not amplified." She doesn't know if she means the prayer or the pattern. She returns to family carrying unease without resolution.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Confused → processing → half-insight (loudspeaker moment) → unresolved → returning to normalcy (surface only; unease carried underneath).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Meera wants to understand her own behavior — why did she protect something she barely understands? Multiple interpretations available to her: family loyalty? Small-town protectiveness? Desire to be the one who understands it (not some journalist)? Paranoia? All valid. None certain. She can't resolve it. The watching-cost is now complicity: she ACTIVELY concealed.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
The impossibility of naming what she's protecting. If she could name it, she could evaluate her choice. But it's formless — two quiet interventions; a feeling; a pattern she suspects but can't prove. You can't explain why you protected something you can't describe.
