## 1. Scene Objective
Escalate the path dispute from internal to interpersonal: a forced polite meeting where both men perform alliance-maintenance while delivering controlled aggression. Tea remains untouched — the social rupture expressed through refused hospitality.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Stiff formality → controlled anger → dread (it's worse than he thought).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Ramesh wants to maintain dignity without conceding; Venki wants to dismiss the path issue as settled; neither will drink the other's hospitality because drinking = accepting the other's frame.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
The meeting's social structure forbids directness — no shouting, no threats, no ultimatums allowed within alliance-maintenance register; the aggression can only emerge through indirection; tea untouched = the only honest signal available.
