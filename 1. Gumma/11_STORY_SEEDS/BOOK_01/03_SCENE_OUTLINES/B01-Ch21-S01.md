## 1. Scene Objective
Introduce Rohan Desai (features journalist) through a casual jatre encounter. He probes about Shankarappa and the path-terms. Meera closes the door instinctively — deflects, lies ("just an uncle-type"), and doesn't fully know why. P-ROHAN opens. DEC-C: Gayatri visible in crowd as scenery only.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Festival warmth → alert (Rohan's question) → protective instinct (body closes before mind decides) → confused unease (why did she protect that?).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Rohan asks about the pattern Meera has been privately noticing. His asking makes her realize she'd rather he didn't know. She deflects — but can't explain to herself why. His interest is genuine and professional, not malicious — which makes her refusal feel stranger to her.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
Rohan's charm and directness (he asks plainly; hard to dodge without being obviously evasive). Meera's own confusion (she's protecting something she can't name). The social setting (Malathi introduced them; she can't be rude). The noise (loudspeakers; crowd; hard to think clearly in the festival chaos).
