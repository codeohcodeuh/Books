## 1. Scene Objective
Show Nav alone on a Saturday night, drafting his resignation letter — cycling between professional surrender, honest anger, and finally: direct clarity. The letter is perfect. His finger hovers over send. He hasn't typed the "To:" address yet. The threshold between deciding and acting.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Restless determination → frustrated cycling → brief warmth (Meera memory, ISS-12 — sharpens loneliness) → clarity → threshold paralysis.

## 3. Scene Conflict
Nav has mentally quit but can't find the tone. Professional = surrender (leaving politely = accepting the theft quietly). Honest = weakness (admitting he was naive). He cycles between versions until he finds a middle: direct, not cruel, naming what happened without begging. But finding the tone doesn't solve the action — the letter is done; the send is not.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
The gap between writing and sending. The gap between internal decision and external consequence. Monday is different if he sends. He's the same person Tuesday if he doesn't. But tonight: the cursor blinks in an empty "To:" field.
