## 1. Scene Objective
THE NOVEL'S FINAL IMAGE. Bus stand. Farooq waves: "Come back, city girl!" Rain commits — monsoon's first real stay. Bus pulls away. Phone pings: Ananya sent a photo — L06 path after rain. Mud. Green. New footprints (farmers walking normally). The path is ordinary. Shared. Unremarkable. Dispute absorbed into normalcy. Phone glow illuminates the image in the moving bus. Meera texts Nav: "On bus. Tell me later." P-LEAVE closed. Book 1 ends.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Departure motion (bus stand; boarding; rain) → Farooq's warmth (brief; warm; "city girl" — familiar now) → bus moving (the grief of motion; village receding; rain on window) → phone ping (Ananya's photo) → the IMAGE (phone glow on mud path = usable normalcy; it worked; the path holds) → grief + hope (she leaves carrying proof the ground held).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Leaving vs wanting to stay. Motion vs stillness. The bus moves regardless of her feelings. The village recedes — she can't stop it. But: the photo proves the peace continues without her. The path is being walked. The ground is holding. That's hope within grief. The conflict resolves into: carrying proof of peace into the city.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
She's on the bus — can't go back. The grief is motion; she can only: sit in it. But the photo (arriving unbidden; Ananya's gift) transforms pure grief into grief-hope. The obstacle (losing the village) is partially answered by: proof the village continues.

## XVII. Drafting Traps
- Narrator labeling the photo as "symbol of peace" (NO — it's just a photo of a path with footprints; the reader supplies the meaning).
- Making Farooq's shout into oracle speech (it's WARM; SIMPLE; "Come back, city girl!" — not wisdom).
- Over-describing the bus journey (this is departure, not travelogue; quick beats; motion).
- The photo being too perfectly composed/described (it's a PHONE PHOTO — slightly off-angle; maybe thumb shadow; the ordinarity of digital image captures real life imperfectly).
- Summarizing the novel in Meera's head (FORBIDDEN — no retrospective; no "these weeks have taught me"; just: the image; the feeling; the motion).
- Shankarappa appearing in any form (he's GONE — Sat 30 May; not here; not on the bus; not in the photo; absent).
- DEC-J: death tone on ANY element (no elegiac "never see again"; no finality; door OPEN).
- Making the Nav text emotional (it's PRACTICAL — "On bus. Tell me later." — that's all; the lightness is the point).
- The phone glow image being over-explained (describe it; let it sit; STOP; the book ends).
- Trying to write a "final thought" or "conclusion" (the IMAGE is the ending; not a sentence of reflection).
