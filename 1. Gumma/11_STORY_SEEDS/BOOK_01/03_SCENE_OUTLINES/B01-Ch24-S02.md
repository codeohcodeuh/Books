## 1. Scene Objective
Aftermath: Meera outside the store, looking toward the bus stand. The traveler is gone. Buses already departed. Dust on the ground shows a hundred footprints — none distinguishable. She can't pursue. ISS-02: she **chooses not to ask Farooq.** ISS-03: watching-cost = complicity/shame (to ask would expose her watching; make her a grabber). She walks back with her rice. P-MSG planted. Chapter closes: short, sharp, unresolved.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Searching (can she spot him?) → frustrated (gone; anonymous; the system worked) → accepting (Ch21 instinct: grabbing breaks things) → cold quiet unease (she's carrying a question she can't ask).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Meera's need to understand against the system's design for invisibility. The traveler is gone. The bus stand is just a bus stand. The hundred footprints on the dusty ground are all the same. The anonymity is not accidental — it's architecture. And she can't break it without breaking something she can't name.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
She can't pursue (gone; which bus? which direction?). She can't ask Farooq (ISS-03: to ask would expose that she was watching — and watching is already its own kind of complicity; asking would make her a grabber of something not offered). She's left with: the weight of what she witnessed, and no outlet.
