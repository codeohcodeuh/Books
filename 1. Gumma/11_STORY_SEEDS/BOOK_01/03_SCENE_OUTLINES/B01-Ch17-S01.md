## 1. Scene Objective
Show shame from the inside. Ramesh processes the Ch16 revelation alone in his sugarcane fields: his "our path" narrative was wrong; his father's word was "let the path be wider than our pride"; he has been the pride. The field beauty exists without offering redemption. The leaf-cut breaks the mental loop through body-pain.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Shame-heavy → field beauty (ISS-12: registering without comfort) → shame-spiral (mental loop) → physical break (cut thumb) → settled heaviness (shame present but peaking has passed).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Ramesh wants to find a way to hold his self-image — but yesterday's words were too precise to deny. The tamarind tree was real. He remembers it. The details are inarguable. He's left with: his position was built on a story he told himself about his father's story. And the real story was: share. He IS what his father cautioned against.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
The specificity of Shankarappa's words (can't deny). The tamarind tree as verification (he knows it existed, knows it fell in '88). His own inability to reframe ("maybe he's wrong?" — but the details prove otherwise). His aloneness — no one to deflect onto, no audience to perform for.
