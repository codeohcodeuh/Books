## 1. Scene Objective
First day of absence (Wed 13 May). Ramesh wakes to a wrong-feeling house. Guest gone since yesterday afternoon. The WhatsApp rumors begin — false forwards about fencing, retaliation, boundary lines. By evening, a Google Maps screenshot with a wrong red line has reached 40 people. DEC-F throwaway delivered (unnamed cousin: "things were quieter when the guest was here"). Ramesh can't counter any of it. Proof by absence — temperature rising.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Shame-paralysis → pressured (noise he can't control) → dread of losing control of a narrative that was never his to control.

## 3. Scene Conflict
Ramesh is trapped between action and inaction. He can't deny the forwards (looks defensive); can't confront anyone (looks aggressive); can't correct the record (his credibility was damaged in Ch16). The vacuum of authority allows rumor to fill the space. No one credible is left to say "this is untrue."

## 4. Scene Obstacle
His credibility is shot (Ch16 humiliation). The WhatsApp ecosystem doesn't respond to corrections — it responds to forwarding. And the one person whose quiet presence seemed to keep temperature down isn't here anymore. Ramesh can barely articulate this — it's a half-thought he doesn't finish.
