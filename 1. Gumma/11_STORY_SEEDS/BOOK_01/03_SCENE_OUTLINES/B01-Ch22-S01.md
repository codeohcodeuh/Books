## 1. Scene Objective
Subbanna's warmth campaign reaches its ask — "you're our man now" — and Shankarappa refuses with cold politeness: "I'm no one's man." He sets his cup down precisely and walks to the guest room. The capture attempt triggers withdrawal. **2 RP (R4).** Meera witnesses and does not chase.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Observant calm → tense (feeling the capture approach) → recognizing a boundary crossed → unsettled (the temperature dropped; something irrevocable just happened).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Subbanna genuinely wants to honor Shankarappa — but his method (claiming, owning, recruiting) is the one thing that will drive Shankarappa away. The conflict is between gratitude-as-capture and autonomy-as-principle. Neither man is wrong in intent — but Subbanna's language ("our man") triggers something absolute.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
Meera can't intervene (she doesn't know the principle at stake). She can't stop Subbanna (his warmth seems kind). She can only watch the temperature drop in real-time and register: something just broke. And she cannot fix it.
