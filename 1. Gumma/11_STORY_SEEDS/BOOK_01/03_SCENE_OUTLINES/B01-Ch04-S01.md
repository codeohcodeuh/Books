## 1. Scene Objective
Force Ramesh from routine morning walk into physical discovery: the fence wire has shifted 6–8 inches, a new post is cemented, and the timing coincides with his absence. Ignite P-PATH as crisis through one man's hands touching fresh concrete.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Routine calm → alert → pride igniting.

## 3. Scene Conflict
Ramesh wanted a normal field inspection; the physical environment confronts him with evidence of deliberate boundary encroachment — done while he was away.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
Cannot confront anyone (alone; dawn; no witnesses); cannot un-see the shift; the cement is evidence of planning — not accident. His body registers it as insult before his mind frames it.
