## 1. Scene Objective
Meera at Farooq's store for errands (rice, dal, soap). Normal banter. A traveler enters — nondescript, dusty canvas bag — waits for another customer to leave, then delivers ONE sentence to Farooq. Opaque. Rehearsed-sounding. Farooq pauses. "Good." Traveler turns and leaves toward bus stand. The exchange takes under 60 seconds. Meera saw everything. **1 RP (R5):** message ecology hinted. P-MSG opens.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Errand-casual → alert (the sentence lands wrong) → cold unease (that wasn't a conversation; that was a communication) → alone with what she saw (Farooq is already back to normal; the world hasn't changed — except she saw something move through a system she can't map).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Meera's pattern-recognition against a system designed to be invisible. She saw something that wasn't FOR her. The sentence was for Farooq. The traveler wasn't selling, buying, or visiting. The entire exchange was: arrival, delivery, acknowledgment, departure. And now she has a question she can't answer without exposing that she noticed.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
She can't ask in the moment (traveler already leaving; Farooq already normal). She can't ask later (ISS-02/ISS-03: asking would expose her watching; would make her a grabber — Ch21 instinct). She's left holding: a glimpse of something that is not hers to see.
