## 1. Scene Objective
Build the meeting through human logistics first (ISS-07), establish Meera's agency (she CHOSE to attend after Ch15 humiliation), show the familiar circles of argument going nowhere — then end on the physical moment when Shankarappa shifts in his chair and the room's temperature changes. The buildup earns the delivery.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Apprehensive → familiar fatigue (heard this before) → courtyard beauty (one breath, ISS-12) → alert shift (something new is about to happen).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Both families have been called together again to "sort it out" — but the familiar positions (Ramesh: "our path"; Venki: "shared access, your side narrowed first") produce nothing new. The conflict is stasis: everyone knows the argument, nobody has new ammunition. Until Ramesh makes his dare — "Let anyone prove when it was different" — expecting silence. And gets: a body-shift from the quiet guest. Meera's watching-cost is now complicity (ISS-03): she's not a wedding guest anymore; she's family who stayed.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
Stasis itself. The meeting threatens to be another pointless circle. Meera's obstacle: she must witness without intervening (learned from Ch15). She can't retreat either (she chose this). She's stuck: watching, implicated, waiting for something she doesn't expect.
