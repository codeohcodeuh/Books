# SCENE B01-Ch13-S01
**Working title:** Mirror, Jasmine, Mine  
**Status:** READY FOR PROSE  
**Version:** 1.0  

---

## 1. Scene Objective
Show Ananya's wedding morning as a field of chaos that she transforms into active choice. She hears Ramesh's tension, feels the cracked ground beneath the day — and chooses to own this morning anyway. The mirror moment: she looks like herself in wedding clothes. That's enough.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Nervous chaos → anger spike (overheard) → active choice → genuine joy (real smile).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Ananya wants her wedding morning to be hers — but the path dispute keeps bleeding through (Ramesh's voice downstairs, the stiffness in logistics, the knowledge that this marriage begins on cracked ground). She must choose joy actively, not receive it passively.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
External: getting-ready chaos (saree pins, jasmine not tied right, forgotten coconut, time pressure). Internal: the anger from Ch5 (overheard fathers) and its ghost — Ramesh's voice this morning saying "no scenes today" confirming that even her wedding is shadowed by the dispute.
