## 1. Scene Objective
Discovery: Shankarappa is gone. Left during afternoon rest (~3:30 p.m. — offstage). Guest room immaculate: mat rolled, blankets stored, chappals absent. Saroja discovers. Lakshmi's one line: "He'll be back or he won't." Meera reads the empty room and is surprised by her own sense of abandonment. P-ABSENCE opens. Act II ends.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Confused alertness (post-S01) → discovery → shock (he actually left) → abandonment (surprises her — she didn't know she cared this much) → settled dread (Act II closing).

## 3. Scene Conflict
The absence itself is the conflict — against expectation, against comfort, against Meera's unspoken hope that she could continue observing him. The room is too clean. He left it as if he was never here. The absence is active — it says something she can't decode.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
She can't ask him why (he's gone). She can't ask Lakshmi (who won't explain — only that one line). She can't chase (ISS-02 — and besides: where?). She's left with: the fact of absence and the feeling it produces. The feeling reveals attachment she didn't know she had.
