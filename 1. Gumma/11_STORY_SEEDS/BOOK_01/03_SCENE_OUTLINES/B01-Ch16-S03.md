## 1. Scene Objective
Immediate aftermath. The room empties. Shankarappa rises (bad knee — ISS-20), sets teacup precisely (Ch12 echo), and leaves without performing. Lakshmi catches Meera's eye (one look — not warning, not invitation; recognition). Meera is left alone with questions she won't ask today. The chapter closes on the silence after specific sentences.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Awed curiosity → approaching the question ("how?") → halted by weight (the moment is too heavy for questions) → settled disappointment + mystery.

## 3. Scene Conflict
Meera wants to understand — to ask "How do you know that?" — but the weight of the moment makes the question feel wrong. Like asking a doctor where they studied while someone is bleeding. The answer isn't the point right now. The effect is. Her curiosity is real and must be held.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
The gravity of what just happened. Asking "how?" would diminish it. Would make her the city-cousin again — treating human depth as research. She can't bring herself to approach Shankarappa OR Lakshmi. She can only: watch them leave. And carry.
