## 1. Scene Objective
Nav alone at the café after Vikram leaves. Coffee cooling. Folder with signed pages. He texts Meera: "Got something. Tell you later." Sent. Delivered. No reply within the chapter (ISS-11: she's packing in village, phone buried). Nav sits with what he got. Recognizes the parallel: partial paper, partial peace. The Ch18 letter stays unsent. Settled regret-dignity.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Post-signing numbness → wanting to tell someone (Meera) → text sent into silence → waiting → the particular loneliness of receiving no response when you have news → settled regret-dignity (he is not the fool who believed; not the hero who burned; the adult who negotiated).

## 3. Scene Conflict
He has news — significant, incomplete news — and no one to share it with right now. Meera won't respond within this chapter (ISS-11: logistics, not neglect). The loneliness of important moments that happen when your people are elsewhere. The parallel forming in his mind (partial paper / partial peace) — a recognition that goes unspoken.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
He can't share this with Meera yet (she won't see the text for hours). He can't celebrate (it's not a celebration — it's a settlement). He can't grieve (he chose this; choosing is not grieving). He's left with: the folder, the rain, the phone, and the knowledge that he's done something irreversible alone.
