## 1. Scene Objective
DEC-A beat: Meera and Ramesh share a quiet moment on the back porch — shared silence, no path talk, no apology, no naming Shankarappa. Shankarappa visible at distance (veranda or guest room area) but NOT participating. Meera's question evolves: "What kind of authority works by sitting?" The house stabilizes. P-GUEST advanced. Earns Ch30 "come sooner next time."

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Post-admission quiet → noticing Ramesh's emergence (relief + shame on his face) → shared silence (unexpected; unplanned; comforting despite everything) → Meera's understanding evolving ("who is he?" → "what IS this?") → settled disappointment-respect (the chapter's landing).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Meera and Ramesh have no language for what they've been through together (the last two weeks; the dispute; the shove; the guest; the absence; the return). They can't talk about it — not yet. But they can stand in the same rain-freshened silence. The conflict is: everything unsaid, held in shared space without requiring resolution.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
She can't bring up the path (not her place; DEC-A). She can't apologize for anything (she hasn't done anything requiring apology). She can't reference Shankarappa (that would make it a three-way conversation). She can only: be present. Stay. Let the silence exist. The obstacle is the same as the gift: there are no words available for this.

## Turning Point

**Irreversible change:** Meera and Ramesh shared forty seconds of silence without speaking, without apologizing, without referencing anything. They just: were. In the same rain-freshened air. After everything. That can't be undone. It's a mark — small, almost invisible, but real. The seed of Ch30. And separately: Meera's question has evolved. She's no longer asking "who is he?" (which could be answered by a document, a name, a history). She's asking "what IS this?" — which can only be answered by watching, over years, what this kind of authority does. A harder question. A better one. The right one.  
**Type:** Coexistence / question-evolution / DEC-A  
**Later breakage if removed:** Ch30 warmth unearned; the DEC-A requirement unmet; Meera's intellectual evolution absent; the house-stabilization invisible.
