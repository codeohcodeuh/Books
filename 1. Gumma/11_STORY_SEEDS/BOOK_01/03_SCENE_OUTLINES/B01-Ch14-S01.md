## 1. Scene Objective
Shatter the wedding-truce by showing a drunk Hegde relative boasting about "our path" in earshot of departing Gowda guests. Meera witnesses the damage happen in real-time — 15 seconds of words that undo the ceremony's goodwill. Deliver DEC-B beat: a peripheral glimpse of Ananya and Karthik in private (gesture only; cracked-ground newlyweds).

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Post-wedding warmth → shock-freeze → deep embarrassment (secondhand cringe; for family, for self, for the illusion she briefly enjoyed).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Meera wants to wind down in post-wedding relief — but a drunk relative's loud mouth destroys the fragile inter-family peace in real-time. She can't stop it (happening too fast), can't fix it (damage done), can't disown it (he's her family). She's implicated by proximity.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
Speed of damage (over before she can react); the drunk relative's obliviousness (he thinks he's being strong, not destructive); Meera's powerlessness in the moment (plates in hand; no social standing to silence an elder-male relative); the Gowda guests' refusal to turn around (which makes it worse — they HEARD but won't give the satisfaction of acknowledging).
