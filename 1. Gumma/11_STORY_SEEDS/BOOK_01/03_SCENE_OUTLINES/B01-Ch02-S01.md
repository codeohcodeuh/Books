## 1. Scene Objective
Force Nav to confront that Vikram has repeated the exact cadence and deferral of the 2021 ESOP promise — in a public corridor where pressing is impossible — leaving him holding warmth that contains nothing.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Nervous hope → deflated unease.

## 3. Scene Conflict
Nav wants Vikram to commit a meeting date to calendar; Vikram wants to exit the corridor without committing anything while maintaining loyalty.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
Vikram's warmth is genuine-seeming, his words are affirming, his deferral is timebound ("after the board deck") — Nav cannot name the dodge without seeming ungrateful; corridor too public; colleague nearby.
