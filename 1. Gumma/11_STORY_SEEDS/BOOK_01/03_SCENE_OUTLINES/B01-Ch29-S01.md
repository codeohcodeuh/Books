## 1. Scene Objective
Meera's last night in the village. Late night. Lakshmi's room. Sitting as when Meera was small — Lakshmi in bed, Meera on floor beside. Meera asks: "Ajji. Shankarappa-avare. Who is he?" Lakshmi's hand on Meera's head. Then the sentence — exact: **"He keeps things from becoming police."** Then: STOP. The sentence was going to continue — Lakshmi's mouth closes on the next word. Deliberately. Choosing to stop. Love expressed as limitation. **2 RP (R7).** P-LAK deepened. The unfinished sentence hangs in the air for the rest of the novel.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Late-night soft intimacy (departure eve; guard down; the two of them alone) → carefully building to the question (weeks of carrying it) → the question asked (vulnerable) → one gift (the sentence — warm, clear, a frame) → the STOP (love choosing limit) → loneliness (loved by someone who won't give what you need) → acceptance (leans into the hand; the unfinished sentence held between them; for now).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Meera needs to understand. Lakshmi knows but won't tell fully. The conflict is between love-as-giving and love-as-withholding. Lakshmi gives one sentence (real; warm; accurate). Then withdraws. Not from coldness — from LOVE (the knowing is dangerous, or burdensome, or not Meera's to carry yet). The sentence that stops is MORE painful than silence would have been — because it proves Lakshmi HAS more. And is choosing not to give it.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
Meera can't push (read Lakshmi's face: this is all you get). She can't trick or pressure (this is her grandmother; love precludes manipulation). She can't wait for more (she leaves Monday). She can only: receive what's offered. Accept the limit. Lean into the hand that won't give her the words. The obstacle IS the love — love that protects by withholding.

**Institutional / RP work:** **2 RP (R7).** Anti-escalation framing given in civilian terms by an adjacent-aware family member. NOT institutional language. NOT structural explanation. One functional sentence: "He keeps things from becoming police." Then: the structure of STOPPING.
