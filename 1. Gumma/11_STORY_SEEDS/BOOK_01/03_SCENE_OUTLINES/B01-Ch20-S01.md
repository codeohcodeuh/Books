## 1. Scene Objective
Introduce Gayatri through her function: she arrives with jasmine, asks one seemingly casual question about the Gowda daughter-in-law, and creates conditions for the mothers (Saroja/Revathi) to commit to a conciliatory gesture (sending pickle). She takes no credit. She leaves with an empty bowl. Meera notices something happened but can barely name it. **2 RP (R3).**

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Domestic routine → mildly alert (who is this woman?) → watchful → unsettled (something just happened and she can barely see it).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Meera observes a room's temperature change through one visit. Gayatri doesn't argue, doesn't mediate, doesn't reference the path. She asks about "Karthik's wife" (Ananya — now married into the Gowda-Hegde tension). This ONE question opens a door for Saroja to say something conciliatory. The conflict is invisible: the reader sees it only through Meera's growing alertness. The mothers genuinely soften — not manipulated; they CHOOSE — but Gayatri created the opening.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
Meera can't articulate what she's seeing. The visit looks entirely normal (a village woman dropping jasmine after temple). Nothing unusual happened on the surface. Yet the room is different after Gayatri leaves. Meera's obstacle: she can't point to the mechanism because there isn't one visible. Only the effect.
