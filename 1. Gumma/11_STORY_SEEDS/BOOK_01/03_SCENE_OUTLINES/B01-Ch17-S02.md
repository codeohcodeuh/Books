## 1. Scene Objective
Arjun calls with the modern solution: "Put it in writing. Get signatures. Legal standing." Ramesh is caught between paper-temptation and his father's principle (which said: no document needed; sons should remember). P-PAPER opens. Ramesh resists — not heroically, but because something in him can't accept paper as substitute for what was broken.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Exposed → tempted (paper sounds like certainty) → resisting (father's words surfacing through him) → isolated (alone again; no solution accepted).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Arjun's paper-solution is rational: boundaries, writing, signatures = certainty. Ramesh WANTS certainty (after yesterday's humiliation). But his father's words keep surfacing: "Let neither of us need a document for what our sons should remember." Accepting paper = admitting the sons failed (which Ramesh now knows they did — but admitting it on paper feels like another layer of shame).

## 4. Scene Obstacle
Arjun's confidence (he's not wrong from his world); the temptation of certainty (paper would END the ambiguity); Ramesh's inability to articulate WHY he resists (it's not rational — it's inherited principle, now activated by shame).
