## 1. Scene Objective
Nav doesn't send. He closes the laptop, makes tea, checks his phone (Meera's 2-day-old text; no Vikram), and goes to bed with the email unsent. The chapter closes on the honest state: he hasn't decided. The letter exists but hasn't entered the world.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Restless → resigned → lonely → asleep with it unresolved.

## 3. Scene Conflict
Send or don't? If he sends: Monday is different; his life changes; bridges burn. If he doesn't: he's the person who drafted and didn't act. That thought — being someone who writes and doesn't execute — is worse than either option. But tonight, he's still that person. Because he's not sending tonight. He's going to sleep.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
The impossibility of resolving tonight. He's alone; can't check with Meera; can't undo the clarity; can't ignore it either. The only honest action is: carry it into tomorrow. The email will be there.
