## 1. Scene Objective
Meera alone in the courtyard after Gayatri's visit. She connects two data points (Shankarappa + Gayatri): two people, two quiet interventions, two rooms that changed temperature without announcement. She almost asks Lakshmi — but chooses not to (ISS-02 agency). DEC-I: Lakshmi passes with opacity + warmth. The chapter closes on unease.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Processing → pattern-alert (two instances form a line) → approaching question → held back (respecting Lakshmi's peace) → unease (settled; carried).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Meera wants to understand what she's seeing: is there a pattern? Two people who arrive quietly and change rooms without claiming credit? But she can't ask without sounding paranoid — and asking Lakshmi might break the peaceful morning. The watching-cost is social risk (ISS-03).

## 4. Scene Obstacle
ISS-03: asking the wrong person could expose suspicion, break Lakshmi's peace, or make Meera look paranoid ("I think there are people who just KNOW things and time their visits" — sounds crazy). Plus: Lakshmi's opacity-warmth is a wall she can't breach without cost. She chooses respect.
