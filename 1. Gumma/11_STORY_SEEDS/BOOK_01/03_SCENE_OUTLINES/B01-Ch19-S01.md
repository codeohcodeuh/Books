## 1. Scene Objective
Meera attends her apartment AGM running on coffee and spite after a night-bus + full workday. The meeting is comic-sharp: petty governance at micro-scale. Through fatigue-stripped eyes, Meera recognizes the pattern: "This is the same fight. Smaller scale. Same structure." CCTV compromise happens through face-saving framing, not through one side proving the other wrong. P-APT truce delivered.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Obligation fatigue → watchful (observing the arguments) → amused recognition (the pattern click) → amusement (settling).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Meera wants to survive this meeting and catch her return bus. But the arguments pull her attention: CCTV proposal, parking rules, maintenance fund — all versions of "shared space, competing claims." She recognizes (through fatigue) that these are the same problem she's been living in the village. The CCTV compromise demonstrates something: face-saving formatting matters more than content. METHOD of resolution > being right.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
Physical: exhaustion from night-bus + workday + coffee crash approaching. Time pressure: return bus tonight. Social: Asha present; Ch1 incident referenced obliquely. Mental: she's too tired for sophistication — which paradoxically makes the pattern-recognition clearer.
