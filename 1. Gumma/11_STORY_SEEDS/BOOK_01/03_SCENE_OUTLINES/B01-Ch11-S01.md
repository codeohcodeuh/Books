## 1. Scene Objective
Show law's tools hitting a wall: Priya takes contradictory statements from both sides, finds no documentation to resolve the dispute, and draws arrows in her notebook that point to a center with no label — sensing something beneath the shove that her instruments cannot reach.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Professional routine → pressing → frustrated → uneasy.

## 3. Scene Conflict
Priya needs clear facts to build a file; both sides' statements contradict; no survey number covers this path; no documentation of shared-use terms exists. Her tools (FIR, statements, survey marks) cannot reach the social roots.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
Neither side is fully lying — both are editing; the path isn't individually registered; "generations" as claim lacks legal weight; her prior communal file failure makes this feel personally dangerous; she smells something older but has no instrument to reach it.
