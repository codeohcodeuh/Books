**Status:** READY FOR PROSE

## 1. Scene Objective
Ananya walks Meera home in silence, then offers one true sentence that neither heals nor accuses. The muddy sandals image closes the chapter. ISS-12 micro-recovery: Ananya's kindness is small comfort — not healing, but human warmth after public humiliation.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Quiet empathy → honest comfort (one sentence) → residual regret (chapter close: the regret doesn't resolve; it's carried).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Ananya wants to comfort Meera but doesn't want to lie. "You tried" isn't quite right — Meera tried the wrong thing (the right thing without the authority to do it). Ananya settles for truth that's also kind: "They're right that you'll leave. They're wrong that it means you don't care."

## 4. Scene Obstacle
The impossibility of the perfect comforting sentence. Every version is either dishonest ("they were wrong") or cruel ("you shouldn't have tried"). Ananya finds the middle: acknowledging truth while insisting on Meera's intention.
