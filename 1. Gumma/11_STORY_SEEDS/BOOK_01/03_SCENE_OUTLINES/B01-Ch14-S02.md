## 1. Scene Objective
Meera alone in the dark, choosing not to tell Ramesh tonight. This is ISS-02 agency: active decision to withhold, not paralysis. She becomes implicated — no longer an observer, not yet a participant. She carries.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Processing → active choice (withhold) → settled embarrassment (body-held; chapter closing on the weight of implication).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Tell or not tell? If she tells → Ramesh angry at cousin, but damage to Gowda relations is done regardless. If she doesn't tell → she carries it alone. The conflict resolves NOT as resolution but as deliberate hold: telling won't undo the boast; it will only add noise tonight.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
The impossibility of fixing: nothing she can do changes what happened. The choice is between two forms of carrying. She picks the quieter one.
