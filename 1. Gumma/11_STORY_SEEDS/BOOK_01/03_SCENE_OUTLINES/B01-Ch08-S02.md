## 1. Scene Objective
Deliver P-DOC misdirection: Suresh pauses, sends a text to someone offstage (his wife — about lunch), places phone face-down. The staging deliberately invites the reader to suspect institutional communication. Content stays hidden.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Routine completion → weight → pause → private unease.

## 3. Scene Conflict
Suresh carries the day's confidences with nowhere to put them; the clinic empties but the weight stays; his text is mundane but his emotional state makes it look heavy.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
No one to debrief with; professional isolation; the text relieves nothing (it's just logistics to his wife); the weight of knowing community pain without any tool to address it.
