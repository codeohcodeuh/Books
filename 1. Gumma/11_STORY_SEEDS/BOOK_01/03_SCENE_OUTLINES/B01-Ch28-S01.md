## 1. Scene Objective
Nav meets Vikram at a neutral café. Vikram slides two-page document across the table: accelerated vesting on reduced shares — not the 2021 oral promise. Less. Visibly less. But real. Dated. Signable. Nav reads. Signs. Both pages. "We're done." P-ESOP closes (partial). Not vindication. Not betrayal. Adult settlement with scar.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Nervous determination (the meeting is today) → reading with cold clarity (the document; the math; the gap between promise and paper) → bitter acceptance (this is what four years of trust gets; it's not zero; it's not enough) → signed regret (the pen scratch; the exhale; the dignity of choosing this over burning it down).

## 3. Scene Conflict
The 2021 oral promise versus what's on paper today. Four years of loyalty versus reduced shares. Vikram's warmth (still genuine) versus the math that never matched. Nav must decide: sign this incomplete thing and live with the scar, or refuse and get nothing (and burn a bridge from which there's no coming back). The conflict is: accepting incompleteness without cruelty.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
He can't get the full 2021 promise (it was oral; never written; it's gone). He can't punish Vikram without punishing himself. He can't walk away with nothing (he's practical; he has rent). He can only: sign what's offered and carry the knowledge that trust broke partially and was partially repaired.
