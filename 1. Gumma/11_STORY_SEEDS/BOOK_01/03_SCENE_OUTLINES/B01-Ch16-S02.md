## 1. Scene Objective
Shankarappa speaks the 1962 shared-use terms — calmly, specifically, with undeniable precision. Both fathers flinch. Neither is vindicated. The truth diminishes both. **3 RP (R2).** The novel's single most important scene.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Anticipation → shock at specificity → cold disappointment (truth doesn't vindicate anyone) → unsettled awe (how does he KNOW this?).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Truth vs both sides' self-narratives. Shankarappa's words make it impossible for either father to claim exclusive right or pure victim status. The conflict is between received history (what families told themselves) and held history (what was actually agreed). Both are diminished. Neither gets what they wanted from this meeting.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
The specificity is the obstacle — to denial. The names (Mallappa Hegde, Chandrappa Gowda), the year (1962), the physical marker (tamarind tree, fell '88 storm), the exact terms (shared use, rotating maintenance, no narrowing below cart-width), the quoted words — all too precise to be invented, too old to be recently manufactured. Neither father can say "that's wrong" because the details are undeniable. They can only: receive.
