## 1. Scene Objective
Show Ramesh absorbing the meeting's failure in isolation — his refusal to explain to Saroja, a brief sighting of Meera (who registers his mood without understanding), and the internal recognition that this dispute has no available resolution mechanism.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Controlled anger → isolation → hardened dread.

## 3. Scene Conflict
Saroja wants information; Ramesh cannot explain that politeness was the aggression; Meera's presence reminds him of normal life continuing around his crisis; the cups remain full as physical evidence of failure.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
He cannot articulate what happened to anyone — the insult was structural not verbal; explaining "he didn't drink my tea" sounds absurd to outsiders; he's alone with a feeling that has no language.
