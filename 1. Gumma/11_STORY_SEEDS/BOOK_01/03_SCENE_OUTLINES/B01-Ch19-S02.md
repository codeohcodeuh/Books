## 1. Scene Objective
Meera passes Asha in the corridor. She chooses the nod — neither smile nor scowl; deliberate minimum; P-APT closed. Then packs, departs for return bus. The least dramatic resolution possible, which makes it real.

## 2. Scene Emotional Objective
Amused settling → practical (packing) → the nod (agency choice) → slightly melancholic (she's changed and knows it).

## 3. Scene Conflict
Minimal external conflict — the fight is over. Internal: Meera could maintain cold distance (safe; familiar) or offer false warmth (dishonest). She chooses the middle: a nod. Deliberate, not accidental. This IS the truce — both women know it without discussing it.

## 4. Scene Obstacle
Nothing dramatic. The obstacle is the internal question: "do I acknowledge her or not?" — answered with the nod. Then: the time pressure of catching the bus. The melancholy of recognizing she's no longer the person who arrived for this AGM months ago.
