## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
The novel's deepest emotional reveal in **intimate/relational** register (ISS-17). Late night, Meera asks Lakshmi directly: who is Shankarappa-avare? Lakshmi gives **one framing sentence** — exact words: **"He keeps things from becoming police."** — and **STOPS**. Mid-thought. Deliberate. Not forgetting. Love expressed as limitation. The unfinished sentence is Book 1's final institutional beat at human scale — deepens mystery while offering one warm frame.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
Lakshmi's arc never culminates; reader never gets one-sentence framing; P-LAK never deepens; Meera–Lakshmi intimacy has no peak; mystery never reaches deepest human form.

**Promise job:**  
P-GUEST deepest point (one sentence + stop). **P-LAK deepened** — she KNOWS and WON'T say. Not closed.

**Emotional job:**  
Loneliness — loved by someone who won't give what you need. Warmth + limitation. Stop hurts because it comes from love, not coldness.

**Institutional / RP job:**  
**2 RP (R7).** On-page: Lakshmi's anti-escalation framing + deliberate stop. Reader: "Family knows a type." Incorrect: she'll finish this book. Correct (writer only): adjacent awareness; protective sacrifice.
