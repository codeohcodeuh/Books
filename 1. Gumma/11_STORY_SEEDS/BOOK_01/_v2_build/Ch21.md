## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Introduce Rohan Desai (features journalist) as the external threat — someone hunting the same pattern Meera has noticed, but with intent to publish. The jatre is the setting: noise, crowd, festival. Rohan approaches Meera (he's heard she's "the city niece who's been asking around") and probes. Meera realizes: he's looking for what she half-sees. But his intent is capture/expose, not understand. She **chooses** to close the door she was opening — and doesn't fully know why. **DEC-C:** Gayatri appears silently in the crowd — scenery only; zero RP, zero dialogue, zero function; Meera may or may not notice; no conclusion; not near Shankarappa pairing.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
Rohan has no introduction; the exposure-threat never plants; P-ROHAN never opens; the reader doesn't see that the pattern (Shankarappa/Gayatri) is visible to others too; Meera's protective instinct toward the village never develops; the jatre atmosphere (noise, crowd, festival) is lost; Gayatri second-sighting seed lost (DEC-C).

**Promise job:**  
P-ROHAN opens — will this journalist discover/expose the pattern? The threat of exposure is now active (series-level; not resolved Book 1).

**Emotional job:**  
Unease — the specific kind when someone asks you about something you were privately wondering about, and their asking makes you realize you'd rather they didn't know.

**Institutional / RP job:**  
**0 RP** by design. No institutional reveal happens. Rohan is STARVED. Gayatri is SCENERY ONLY (DEC-C). Doors close in his face. But his hunting validates that the pattern is real (attentive reader notes: if there's nothing, why are doors closing?).
