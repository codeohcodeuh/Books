## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Prove by negative space that Shankarappa's presence was functional. During his three-day absence, rumor escalates, the path dispute heats up again (new WhatsApp forwards claiming Hegde-side is "planning retaliation"), community temperature rises. No one connects this to the quiet man's departure in thesis form — but the reader should feel: when he was here, this didn't happen. **DEC-F:** One dismissible throwaway civilian line max ("things were quieter when the guest was here"); timing/temperature carry the argument. **ISS-23:** Compressed montage with one near-miss (almost second confrontation / almost new complaint) that does NOT happen.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
The absence has no consequence; Ch25 return has no relief-contrast; the reader never feels what the institution prevents; the "proof by absence" argument (core to the novel's structure) is lost.

**Promise job:**  
P-ABSENCE advances — the absence teaches.

**Emotional job:**  
Dread. The slow kind: watching a situation deteriorate when the invisible brake has been removed.

**Institutional / RP job:**  
0 RP. Shankarappa is absent. The institutional layer is proved by its absence — not by any present action. This is structural argument, not textual reveal.

The guest left yesterday afternoon while Ramesh was in the back field. Ramesh registers: "the guest left." He's not tracking it as meaningful — he's too inside his own shame.

**Unresolved pressure entering:**  
Shankarappa absent (since Tue ~3:30 p.m.); mothers' bridge-building fragile; police file still open; no formal resolution; vacuum.

**Reader assumptions currently true:**
