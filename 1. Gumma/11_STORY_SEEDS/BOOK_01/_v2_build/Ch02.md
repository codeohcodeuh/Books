## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Introduce Nav and the second strand of the thematic rhyme: oral promises in professional life. Plant the theme ("words spoken informally still rearrange lives") without institutional framing. Bring Meera into workplace context so readers see her duality (competent PM vs. last night's parking embarrassment). End with her witnessing Nav's unease — connecting them as co-observers of promises that may not hold.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
Nav has no introduction; ESOP rhyme has no setup; Ch.18 and Ch.28 payoffs arrive without reader investment; thematic bridge between apartment rules and village agreements loses its middle term; Meera's work persona is unknown.

**Promise job:**  
P-ESOP moves from background to active reader concern. Nav had the promise in 2021; today Vikram re-airs it vaguely without committing — the promise becomes shakier precisely as it appears to be repeated.

**Emotional job:**  
Unease. The reader should feel the specific discomfort of hearing a boss perform generosity in a hallway while dodging the document that would make it real.

**Institutional / RP job:**  
0 RP. No invisible layer. No mystery. Corporate oral promises as the urban face of the novel's deeper question about agreements that bind.

**Forbidden lore:**

**Invisible layer limits:**  
Zero. Absent entirely.

**POV restrictions:**  
Close-third Nav throughout. Meera perceived externally in S01 (background); she becomes conversational equal in S02 but still Nav's POV governs. No access to Meera's interior (reader gets her interior in her own chapters).

**Dialogue restrictions:**  
Vikram speaks in executive warmth — short, affirming, timebound ("after the board deck"; "lock it"). Nav speaks in careful, prepared phrases that crumble on contact. Meera speaks practically in café — testing, not counseling. No one philosophizes about trust or promises.

**Allowed ambiguity:**  
Whether Vikram is malicious or merely avoidant — reader may read either. Story does not need to resolve this (Ch.28 will partially).
