## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Shatter the wedding-truce illusion. A drunk male relative (Hegde side) boasts about "our path" and "won't give an inch" in earshot of departing Gowda-side guests. The peace was performance. Post-wedding alcohol removes the mask. Meera witnesses and feels embarrassment — for her family, for the illusion she briefly enjoyed. Same night: a brief peripheral glimpse of Ananya and Karthik's lived marriage under strain (DEC-B) — cracked ground as newlyweds, not abstract theme. Meera makes a concrete choice: she will not tell Ramesh tonight.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
Reader believes wedding-truce was real; path dispute seems resolved; the escalation curve post-wedding lacks its trigger; Meera's growing awareness of the dispute's depth doesn't advance; marriage-on-cracked-ground stays abstract without DEC-B beat.

**Promise job:**  
P-PATH advances — the truce was temporary. The path is back.

**Emotional job:**  
Embarrassment — secondhand. The cringe of watching your family member destroy something fragile while thinking they're being strong.

**Agency beat (ISS-02):**  
Meera **chooses not to tell** Ramesh about the boast tonight — holds the knowledge alone rather than escalate or perform helpfulness.

**Institutional / RP job:**  
0 RP. Purely civilian. This is what happens without custodianship — truces are performances that collapse when the performer is tired/drunk.
