## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Introduce Gayatri — the second institutional presence — through her function, not her identity. She "drops by" the Hegde house with jasmine flowers (a normal village-women thing), and in the course of a casual visit, somehow softens the mothers' mood toward Gowda-side. She doesn't mediate. She doesn't argue. She adjusts TIMING — arrives when the women are vulnerable-open (post-morning chores, tea time, no men present), brings beauty (jasmine), and creates conditions for the mothers to say conciliatory things they wouldn't have said unprompted. Meera notices: this is the second time she's seen quiet competence do work in this village. Unease. **Human-first:** morning chores and tea before any institutional texture arrives.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
Gayatri has no introduction; the Connector function never demonstrates; the novel loses its evidence that there might be a NETWORK (Shankarappa + Gayatri = pattern, not anomaly); P-GAYA never opens; the women's sphere of conflict-resolution stays invisible; Lakshmi mid-book presence vanishes (DEC-I).

**Promise job:**  
P-GAYA opens — who is she? Why did she show up at exactly the right moment? Is she connected to Shankarappa?

**Emotional job:**  
Unease — the growing kind. Meera recognizing a pattern: two instances of quiet competence changing a room's temperature. Once (Ch16) could be a person. Twice is a pattern. The unease is: "What am I looking at?"

**Institutional / RP job:**  
**2 RP (R3).** On-page: Gayatri timing softens mothers. Reader interprets: "Helpful relative/friend." Incorrect: Random coincidence, or she's police-adjacent. Correct (writer only): Connector function — she creates conditions for de-escalation without claiming credit.

## 10. Drafting Constraints

**Prose must avoid:**  
Making Gayatri atmospheric/mysterious in narrator voice; revealing her deliberation; making mothers' softening look manipulated (it's genuine — Gayatri only created conditions); Meera's suspicion becoming certainty; opening on institutional texture before chores/tea.

**Forbidden exposition:**  
Connector function; network; Gayatri's purpose.

**Forbidden lore / vocabulary:**  
All Appendix B. Also: Connector · timing · function · network · planned · deliberate · staged · arranged · her purpose · the real reason · she came because

**Invisible layer limits:**  
**2 RP.** Function demonstrated through action (arrival, question, jasmine), not through labeling. Gayatri's interior NEVER shown. Narrator cannot know why she's there. Only effects are visible.

**POV restrictions:**  
Close-third Meera. Gayatri entirely external (actions, words, smile). Saroja/Revathi external. Lakshmi: external only — warmth + opacity, no interior.

**Dialogue restrictions:**  
Gayatri speaks in warm village-woman register (casual, generous, unhurried). The key question ("How is Karthik's wife settling?") must feel natural — one sentence among many. Saroja responds warmly. No one discusses the path directly.

**Watching-cost constraint (ISS-03):**  
Meera's withheld question costs her **social risk** — she could look paranoid or break Lakshmi's peace. Do not merge with other chapters' watching registers.

**Allowed ambiguity:**  
Whether Meera is right about the pattern; whether Lakshmi knows Gayatri.
