## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Shankarappa is offered capture (uncle Subbanna tries to own/flatter him into becoming "our side's" memory-man) and he responds by LEAVING. For three days. This is the Act II ending: the mystery deepens via absence. The reader (and Meera) now understand: whatever he is, he cannot be owned. His value is demonstrated by his refusal to be instrumentalized. The empty guest mat is the image that closes Act II. **ISS-19:** Subbanna's warmth campaign pays off here (planted Ch12+). **Meera agency:** she witnesses and accepts — does not chase.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
Shankarappa's non-extraction principle never demonstrates; the three-day absence (Ch23) has no cause; Act II has no ending; the institutional principle that custodians cannot be captured is never shown; P-ABSENCE never opens; Subbanna feels schematic without prior warmth plant.

**Promise job:**  
P-ABSENCE opens (where is he? will he return?). P-GUEST advances dramatically (he refused capture — he has principles Meera can't name).

**Emotional job:**  
Unease → abandonment. The specific dread of losing something you barely understood before it disappeared.

**Institutional / RP job:**  
**2 RP (R4).** On-page: relative tries to flatter/own; Shankarappa refuses and leaves. Reader interprets: "He's proud/offended." Incorrect: ego bruised. Correct (writer only): non-extraction principle — a Holder cannot be instrumentalized by either side or the function is corrupted.
