## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Close the police thread with anticlimax — which IS the point. The state can document but not resolve. Priya closes the path file after both parties decline to pursue (word relayed through relatives, not formal affidavits). The stamp is crooked; the ink pad was dry. She doesn't re-stamp. It's good enough. The file goes to the shelf. Social peace arrived on the path (Ch26) before paper caught up. This chapter shows: law follows social reality, never leads it.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
P-POLICE never closes; Priya's arc has no completion; the state-thread dangles; the contrast (social resolution preceded legal closure) never concretizes; Ch11 notebook-arrows setup pays off nowhere.

**Promise job:**  
P-POLICE **closed**. Anticlimax is the form — not justice, not failure, just late paperwork.

**Emotional job:**  
Relief — the mundane kind. The exhale of bureaucracy doing its small, late thing after the village has already moved on.

**Institutional / RP job:**  
0 RP — civilian only. Priya is state apparatus, not network. No invisible layer. **DEC-D:** P-DOC remains intentionally unresolved; this chapter must not banalize or tidy the doctor-text itch from Ch8.

## 3. Opening State

**POV entering emotion:**  
Professional routine with undertow of fatigue. The path file has sat open since Ch11. Priya expected escalation; instead the village went quiet in a way her forms cannot capture.

**POV entering knowledge:**  
Priya knows: contradictory statements on record (Ch11); path visited; shared-use, no exclusive documentation; both families warned; wedding happened; shove incident aging; recent word (via Saroja-adjacent relative and a Gowda-side messenger — not formal) that neither side wants "police matter" to continue. She suspects the fight cooled without her.

**Other characters — entering emotions:**
- Ramesh (offstage): relieved the file may close; won't come to PS unless summoned
- Venki side (offstage): same — managing down
- Farooq (later): ordinary cheer; store rhythm restored
- PSI (offstage pressure): wants communal-sensitive files cleared before transfer season

**Unresolved pressure entering:**  
Open FIR still technically active; PSI review pending; Priya's **private stake** (ISS-18 payoff): three years ago she mishandled a smaller communal file — two families, a festival boundary, her premature "closed" notation before tempers cooled; one side escalated to district; she received a warning and a stalled transfer application. She cannot afford another "closed too early" OR "left open too long" mark on her record. This path file is her chance to close cleanly.

**Reader assumptions currently true:**  
Police may still matter; someone official will deliver verdict; Priya might discover deeper history.

**Reader assumptions we will not correct this chapter:**  
That Priya will learn 1962 terms or institutional layer. That P-DOC (Ch8 doctor text) connects to this file. **DEC-D:** doctor itch stays unresolved — no wink, no banalization.
