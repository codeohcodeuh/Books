## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
The path dispute resolves — not through law, not through documents, not through victory. Through bodies. Ramesh and Venki (and others) walk the path on the same morning after rain, and their footprints overlap. Nobody announces peace. Nobody signs anything. They just USE it. Together. The wet earth records what words couldn't: shared use restored. **P-PATH closes as "usable."** **DEC-E / ISS-16:** P-PAPER closes — Ramesh does not call Arjun; no papering; max one unspoken memory flicker OR Ananya noticing absence of officials/documents; NO speech about paper on path. **Shankarappa ABSENT from path walk** — people make peace themselves. **ISS-21:** Sandals/prints callback from Ch15 muddy sandals.

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
P-PATH never resolves; P-PAPER left open; the novel's central argument (usable peace > legal victory) never enacts; Ananya's POV on the resolution (the bride who watched her wedding poisoned by this fight) is lost; the footprint-image never arrives.

**Promise job:**  
**P-PATH closes as usable.** Not victory. Not justice. Shared use restored through bodies, not decree. The 1962 terms are LIVED INTO rather than formalized. **P-PAPER closes** as choice against pure paper (DEC-E).

**Emotional job:**  
Relief — the incomplete kind. The exhale that comes from watching stubborn men share a space without acknowledgment. Not warm. Not healing. Just: not fighting. For now. That's enough.

**Institutional / RP job:**  
0 RP. Shankarappa is NOT present for this walk. He doesn't need to be. His function was: speak the truth and create conditions for bodies to find their own arrangement. The resolution happens without him. THAT is the point — the institution doesn't DO the peace. It enables the CONDITIONS for peace. People make peace themselves.
