## 2. Chapter Purpose

**Why this chapter exists:**  
Parallel the village story with its urban mirror. Nav drafts his resignation letter — the moment where loyalty breaks and agency asserts itself. But he doesn't send it. The chapter is about the space between deciding and acting, between the draft and the send button. It rhymes with Ramesh's field-walk (processing a broken narrative) and with the novel's theme (promises that exist in the air between people, never papered or retracted).

**Removal test (what breaks if deleted):**  
Nav's arc has no crisis point; Ch.28 resolution arrives without the resignation-draft precedent; the urban-rural thematic parallel loses its midpoint; the novel's oral-promise theme loses its corporate mirror at maximum tension.

**Promise job:**  
P-ESOP advances to crisis — Nav has mentally quit but hasn't executed. The promise (ESOP) is now actively refused internally but not yet formally confronted.

**Emotional job:**  
Loneliness. The specific kind of being alone with a decision that changes everything, with no one to check it with (Meera is in the village; he's processing alone).

**Institutional / RP job:**  
0 RP. Purely civilian. Thematic rhyme only (oral promises; loyalty without instruments; the gap between what's said and what's binding). No institutional content.

## 3. Opening State

**POV entering emotion:**  
Saturday-evening restlessness. Meera's been gone weeks (village for wedding). Nav has been stewing since Ch.2 corridor moment. The ESOP clarity meeting never happened. Vikram postponed twice more. Nav has hit his limit.

**POV entering knowledge:**  
Nav knows: 4+ years of loyalty; promise made 2021; Series A happened 2023; equity still unpapered; Vikram's warmth is performance. He's been looking at market salaries. The gap is large. He could leave and earn more. But: the promise. The sunk cost. The team he built. Meera texted two days ago: "Things wild here. Tell you later." — he didn't push.

**Other characters — entering emotions:**  
- Vikram (offstage): oblivious; probably socializing; not thinking about Nav
- Meera (offstage): in village dealing with path drama (Ch16-17); unreachable emotionally tonight
- Nav's flat: empty; his own company

**Unresolved pressure entering:**  
Ch2 corridor (promise re-aired, dodged); months of avoidance since; Meera absent (no sounding board).
